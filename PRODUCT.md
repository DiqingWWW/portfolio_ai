# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, design leaders, and collaborators evaluating Diqing Wu's product-design practice, systems thinking, HMI experience, design-system work, and AI-assisted building capability.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents professional and independent work as evidence-led project narratives inside an experiential workspace. Success means a visitor can understand the designer's role, decisions, system thinking, and evidence without the interface obscuring the work.

## Positioning

The portfolio itself is part of the evidence: a spatial project-discovery experience is paired with conventional, traceable case studies and a governed content workflow that preserves design intent across source material, localized content, and production code.

## Operating Context

Project facts originate in owner-approved Obsidian masters. A repository sync produces selection records and approved assets; localized TypeScript modules author the website narrative from those selected source units; project-specific React components own composition and presentation. The owner reviews changes on localhost before release.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage remains an experiential workspace with a conventional Selected Work path.
- Projects, tags, assets, case-study content, and site-level content remain separate responsibilities.
- Case studies are normal scrolling webpages whose visual evidence accounts for at least half of composed narrative units.
- Public claims, metrics, roles, dates, clients, and outcomes must be traceable to approved source material.
- The current site is bilingual, but individual new research work may launch Chinese-first when the owner explicitly approves that scope.
- New work must preserve current behavior and avoid unrelated redesign or architectural migration.

## Brand Commitments

The product is Diqing Wu's professional portfolio. Its voice is precise, authored, evidence-led, and candid about uncertainty. The warm workspace canvas, restrained editorial case-study layer, Workspace Blue interaction voice, Inter content typography, and JetBrains Mono system annotations are incumbent commitments documented in `DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing portfolio implementation and project records in this repository.
- Product and project-content governance under `governance/`.
- Owner-authored Chinese research notes and two supporting diagrams for the “自动驾驶到 Agent” project, supplied on 2026-09-13.
- No verified quantitative safety comparison, user study, regulatory synthesis, or universal industry consensus has been supplied for that research; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence leads; interaction and styling clarify the work rather than substitute for it.
2. Preserve traceability from factual source to selection, localization, and presentation.
3. Keep human judgment visible in AI-assisted work, including uncertainty and decision boundaries.
4. Prefer bounded, reversible changes over whole-site regeneration.
5. Preserve accessible, responsive comprehension across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, touch-appropriate controls, reduced-motion support, readable responsive typography, and complete inline overviews for diagrams and tables.
